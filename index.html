Frequently Asked Questions

Which areas does Manzil Homes serve?

Manzil Homes is based in Visakhapatnam (Vishalakshi Nagar) and works across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, including Hyderabad, Secunderabad, Gachibowli, Kondapur, Jubilee Hills, Banjara Hills, Kokapet, Warangal, Vijayawada, Guntur, Amaravati, Tirupati and Nellore.

What services does Manzil Homes offer?

Luxury home interiors for apartments, villas and penthouses; architecture and house or villa construction; renovation and remodeling; modular kitchens and wardrobes; theme-based and artistic interiors; 3D visualization; and commercial interiors for offices, cafés and showrooms.

Do you handle both construction and interiors?

Yes. Manzil Homes is a design-and-build company, so one team takes your project from concept and 3D design through construction, interiors and final handover.

Are your designs Vastu compliant?

Yes. Every layout is planned with Vastu principles from the first discussion, balanced with modern, luxury design.

How does the design and build process work?

There are four stages: Discovery (lifestyle, Vastu inputs and budget), Concept and 3D (mood, materials and 3D renders), Detailing (technical drawings, joinery and lighting) and Build and Handover (site execution, quality checks and on-time handover with after-care).

Is your pricing transparent?

Yes. You get a clear bill of quantities (BOQ) with tracked variations and no hidden costs, and work follows milestone-based schedules.

Who leads Manzil Homes?

Manzil Homes is a women-led company founded by Shaik Miza Mehroz, a Civil Engineer and Interior Designer (B.Tech, M.Tech Structures) with over five years of experience in design and interiors.
